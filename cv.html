education

Since 2024 I have been a PhD student in SUSTEEMS (Sustainability: Economics, Environment, Management and Society) at the University of Trento, under the supervision of Silvia Ferrini and Carlo Fezzi. My thesis is on the economic valuation of marine ecosystems: applications, developments and challenges. I am also a member of the university’s Council of Doctoral Students and Holders of Research Contracts.

I hold a Master’s degree in Environmental Economics and Sustainability from the University of Siena (2020–2023, 110/110 cum laude). My thesis, a meta-regression analysis of discrete choice experiments on consumers’ attitudes towards the sustainability of finfish products in the European market, was among the Top 100 Master’s theses of the 2023 Symbola Prize “10 Thesis for Sustainability”.

Before that, I obtained a Bachelor’s degree in Political Sciences from the University of Naples Federico II (2015–2020, 110/110 cum laude), with a thesis on degrowth and criticism to development.

In spring 2026 I was a visiting PhD student at EDHEC Business School (Data Science, Economics & Finance, Nice campus, April–May 2026). I attended the ECOBAS–EAERE Summer School 2026 on recent applications of AI in environmental and resource economics (Santiago de Compostela) and the OGS Summer School in Sustainable Blue Economy (2023). During my Master’s I spent four months at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) as an Erasmus student.

research

My research focuses on the economic valuation of ecosystem services through stated preference methods — discrete choice experiments, contingent valuation and basket- and expenditure-based choice experiments (BEBCE) — and on choice modelling. I am particularly interested in the methodological challenges and behavioural biases of these methods and in consumers’ preferences in sustainable markets. Part of my research also focuses on cost-benefit analysis and on causal inference about the effects of climate change.

My work is connected to two Horizon Europe projects on marine ecosystems. Within MARBEFES (GA 101060937) I am developing a stated-preference survey on the ecosystem services of the Gulf of Gdańsk, with the Institute of Oceanology of the Polish Academy of Sciences. Within OCEAN CITIZEN (GA 101093910) I was a research collaborator at CoNISMa, the Italian Inter-University Consortium of Marine Sciences (2023–2024), working on the economic valuation of ecosystem services and of the impact of restoration activities.

At the University of Trento I am a member of the Applied Environmental and Energy Economics Lab (A3EL). I am also a member of the European Association of Environmental and Resource Economists (EAERE) and of the Italian Association of Environmental and Resource Economists (IAERE).
